Golf and other facilities

Golf

The magnificent Jack Nicklaus Signature golf course is ranked among the best courses in South Africa and will present you with challenging golf and breathtaking surroundings.

You can use the driving range prior to your round. As a non-member, green fees at Pearl Valley are quite steep. Please pre-book your golf directly with us in advance to be sure of discounted rates, and to avoid disappointment on arrival.

Swimming pools

You are welcome to use the pool complex at the Mantis Hotel. Please bring your pool towel — towels are also provided in the lodge.

Access to the pool at the Clubhouse is via fingerprint. You will need to have registered for biometric access before using the swimming pools (only for stays of 4 nights and more).

There are three different pool sizes/depths according to children’s ages and swimming abilities, including pool slides.

There is no lifeguard on duty at the pool.

On the estate

The Valley Restaurant and Bar

Whether you’re enjoying breakfast, lunch or dinner, The Valley Restaurant in the Clubhouse is a good choice. Large windows look over the Jack Nicklaus Signature golf course and the towering Simonsberg Mountain.

Hours: Monday–Sunday 07:00–22:00

Back’s Restaurant and Deli

Situated at Pearl Valley Hotel by Mantis. Familiar fresh goods plus a breakfast, lunch and dinner menu. The deli is stocked with sustainably farmed foods: free-range meat, braai wood, eggs, dairy, condiments, olive oil, jams and artisanal bread.

Mon–Sat 08:00–20:00
Sunday 08:00–15:00 (last order 14:30)

Boma and Equestrian Centre

The Boma is down by the river — great for early morning walks and special evening events throughout the year. Next to the Boma is the equestrian centre if you would like an outride during your stay.

At the lodge

WiFi

Fibre WiFi with a fair usage policy.

DSTV

Each lodge has a DSTV decoder on the Hotel / Family Viewing package — about 20 channels including movies, kids’ cartoons and BBC News. You are welcome to use Netflix or log in with your own account.

Fireplace

There is a gas fireplace in each lodge. In winter we will set up a log fire in advance of arrival for you to light. Please bring additional wood if you would like log fires throughout your stay.

Braai

There is a built-in wood or gas braai on the patio at each lodge. Please bring wood or charcoal if you wish to braai.

Cleaning

A refresh clean or regular daily cleaning can be arranged for an additional fee. Please arrange this at least two days in advance to secure cleaning staff.

Check-out

Check-out is usually at 10:00 on the morning of departure. Please let us know your check-out time so we can arrange for the keys to be picked up.

This is a privately owned lodge and someone’s holiday home. Please leave it in the condition you received it.

Shopping & groceries

At the Mantis Hotel you will find Back’s Deli with sustainably farmed foods.

Drakenstein Centre

About 10 minutes from the estate, between Paarl and the estate. Turn left on the R301 out of Pearl Valley.

Paarl Mall

Continue along the R301 towards the N1, go straight under the bridge to the first set of traffic lights. Turn left and drive parallel to the motorway until you reach the centre — pretty much everything you will need.

Delivery to the lodge

  • Checkers Sixty60 delivers to the estate. Download their app to order.
  • Woolworths in Paarl Mall does daily deliveries to Pearl Valley. Order at woolworths.co.za.

Restaurants nearby

For the best local restaurants, head to Franschhoek — about a 15-minute drive by turning right out of Pearl Valley. It is often described as the food and wine capital of South Africa.

Restaurants in Franschhoek are generally very busy. Book in advance — most have online reservations.
